# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful. Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination and unwelcome sexual attention have no place here. Assume good faith, give useful feedback, and accept it graciously.

Maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributions that break this code, and may ban people who repeatedly do. To report a problem, email **conduct@agent-identity.dev**. Reports are handled privately.
